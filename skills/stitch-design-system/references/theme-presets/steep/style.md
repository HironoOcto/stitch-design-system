## style-paragraph
The canvas is warm paper built as layers, not a flat fill: a full-bleed gradient bitmap of warm-white into white sits behind the content, an SVG turbulence-noise grain lays over it, and mix-blend overlay presses the grain back so the paper carries faint texture and light rather than dead white. Serif Signifier headlines at weight 400 float across it at 44/64/90px with tight tracking, and each display heading carries exactly one italic emphasis phrase — the serif makes its accent by leaning into italic, never by going bold. The system stays near-achromatic apart from a single warm peach accent used at most once per page as an editorial callout card (with sienna-brown ink) and never as a background; the lone chromatic rhythm is a full-bleed near-black AI band, neutral not tinted, joined to the light above it by a white-to-transparent fade seam so the page beats light, then dark, then light. Content cards sit at 24px radius with hairline borders and no drop shadow, while the floating product artifacts earn elevation — region tables, activation charts, and a signature frosted-glass composer with backdrop blur and an inset near-white top highlight — arranged as a scaled, slightly rotated collage with roughly ten-percent shadows rather than a gridded shell, beneath a frosted translucent nav. Buttons are fully pill-shaped, pairing a filled pill with a ghost pill on the same row, and links carry an arrow suffix instead of an underline at rest. Spacing runs on a 4px unit with 80px section gaps, giving the whole page the generous breathing room of a magazine layout.
