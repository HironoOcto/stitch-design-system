## description
Build React UIs in the Phantom style — a flat lavender-near-white candy shop where a full-width hero video leads into a bento grid of solid-color tiles (light candy tints plus near-black obsidian and deep aubergine darks), each tile wearing one faint violet ambient glow. Whisper-weight display type (350) at 80–96px with tight negative tracking and no italic, capsule geometry from 24px tile corners to 100px pill nav and buttons, and a ghost mascot replacing a vowel in headlines.

## style-paragraph
The whole page floats on one flat lavender-near-white plane — no gradient mesh, grain, blur, or background image, the mood coming from hue rather than texture. The hero is a single full-width, rounded, muted auto-looping video; past it the layout returns to a bento grid of equal rounded tiles at 24px radius, each one a solid fill cycling through candy tints (lavender, cornflower blue, cream, butter, blush, fog) interleaved with occasional near-black obsidian and deep aubergine dark tiles, so dark lives only at tile scale and never as a full-bleed band. Every tile — light or dark — wears the same faint violet ambient glow, a 4px zero-spread halo that is the system's only shadow; dark tiles carry near-white on-dark text and sit in the same rows as the light ones. Typography stays whisper-weight at 350, never bolder, with -0.025em tracking at every size and display lines reaching 96px at line-height near 1.0, taking presence from scale and whitespace rather than weight or italic. Navigation, buttons, and tags collapse into 100px pills while cards and containers stay soft at 24px and up, and a periwinkle ghost mascot inlines in place of a vowel in headlines as the one recurring brand device.
