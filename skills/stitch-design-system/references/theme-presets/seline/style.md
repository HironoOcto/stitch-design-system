## description
Build React UIs in the Seline style — a dead-flat warm-stone paper canvas with a single soft sky-blue accent used once per headline as a pill highlight wash. Headlines are Roobert at weight 400 with tight negative tracking (never bolder); body is small Inter on generous line-height. Flat white cards stand on 1px stone hairline borders, screenshots and cards scale down and rotate a few degrees into a loosely stacked collage, and exactly one floating product preview earns a deep, soft neutral shadow.

## style-paragraph
The page sits on a warm-stone paper canvas that stays dead flat — no full-bleed bitmap, no grain, no mesh or colored gradient; the only gradient anywhere is a single achromatic near-white-to-transparent fade softening block edges. Roobert carries every headline at weight 400 with tight negative tracking at roughly 32px and 52px sizes, leaning on scale rather than bold weight, while body copy runs small Inter at weight 400 on a loose ~1.64 line-height for the dominant reading rhythm. The whole chromatic budget is one soft sky-blue: a single phrase per headline wears a sky-blue pill background with blue text, and it never appears in body copy or nav — the pill-shaped cyan CTA is the only chromatically filled control on screen. Content cards are flat white surfaces that stand on 1px stone hairline borders and never take a shadow or frosted glass; product screenshots and feature cards are scaled to about 0.8–0.9, each rotated a few degrees (~2–8°) and nudged horizontally so they layer into a loose collage like stacked stickers, with images treated grayscale and slightly reduced in contrast so they read as monochrome material. Exactly one floating main product preview earns elevation, from a single deep, soft, large-spread neutral shadow (roughly 0 12px 45px to 0 4px 80px in magnitude, never a colored glow), and a grayscale outline mascot sticker peeks from behind a card, slightly rotated with a soft drop shadow, once per region and never animated. Frosted translucent glass is chrome-only — the sticky top nav and small status badges wear about 60% white with ~12px blur — and there is no dark band, no inverted full-bleed section, and no heading italic anywhere; the calm comes from restraint held everywhere but that one blue mark.
